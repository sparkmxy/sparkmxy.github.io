About this deck, randomness & privacy

All 78 cards follow the Rider–Waite–Smith sequence. The images are full scans of the original 1909 Roses & Lilies edition; the brief bilingual notes are original interpretations. Shuffling uses Web Crypto and unbiased sampling, without replacement. Reversals have equal probability when enabled. This is a reflective experience, not a prediction of definite events.

To choose a default language, the page may query Country.is for your network’s country. That service receives your IP, never your question or cards. Only the country code is used; your IP is not stored. Manual language choice takes priority. If lookup fails, browser language is used.

Only readings you explicitly save enter this browser’s local journal. AI answers are not saved or exported. Clearing browser storage removes the journal.
